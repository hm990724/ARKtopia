# Story continuity audit

This audit keeps the active story rules in `STORY_BIBLE.md` and the original source record in `docs/ORIGINAL_ORIGIN_ZERO_STORY.txt`.

- The forced disposal order and containment/power failure cause the pathogen release.
- M-00 escapes separately and is not the source of the outbreak. Its early CCTV shadow is a false lead that resolves to an ordinary cat.
- Major truths remain gated by recovered records, positive specimen results, and late research milestones; calendar day alone does not disclose them.
- Crew histories, losses, and final decisions must be derived from the save state.
- Gameplay changes preserve existing ending routes and ending data. Ending, cinematic, and epilogue artwork is out of scope.
