# Story bible: ORIGIN ZERO

This file is the current narrative entry point. The original source narrative is preserved in `docs/ORIGINAL_ORIGIN_ZERO_STORY.txt`; the gameplay implementation must keep its cause-and-effect canon intact.

## Canonical facts

- The outbreak was caused by a forced disposal order that disabled containment during an unstable experiment. The resulting power and containment cascade released the fictional pathogen.
- M-00, the ordinary black cat, escaped in the resulting chaos. It did not cause the outbreak and must not be falsely blamed by an early reveal.
- Story disclosures unlock from recovered evidence and research progress rather than revealing the origin in the opening days.
- Staff memories and relationships are individual and stateful. Events and endings should reflect actual survival, transfers, trust, and player choices.
- ARK RECALL revisits previously secured specimen identities; it does not replace the original capture or discovery history.

## Gameplay boundaries

The ending and cinematic art, epilogues, and their asset routes are maintained separately by the user. Gameplay work may preserve ending logic and save fields but must not edit those visuals.
