# Artwork handoff

Artwork and cinematic files are outside the current gameplay implementation scope. Existing assets and code-to-asset routes are preserved; do not generate, replace, rename, compress, or remove any image during this task. The current game uses existing species, field, staff, story, and ending assets through their existing resolvers.

Asset provenance files are retained as historical references in `docs/generated-assets-v75.json` and `docs/generated-assets-v75-endings-events.json`. They do not certify that every visual slot is complete. Ending, cinematic, and epilogue artwork remains owned by the user.
