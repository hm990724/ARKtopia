# ORIGIN ZERO — current story canon
The war virus kills human bodies while animals resist it. The scientist joins wartime medical research to save his daughter, fails, preserves her neural records in EVE, and plans a temporary animal host until vaccination permits a return to human bodies. M-00 is the first adapted host candidate: her beloved black cat. Its original brain is intact, no human brain was implanted. Trial hosts already carried human brains; their stability tests were incomplete. The scientist rushes M-00's last experiment, sees independent cognition, dismisses its significance, and the military attack destroys the facility and the daughter's preserved brain. Other trial hosts escape and spread a fictional second, worse infection. The scientist breaks down, orders emergency continuation, and his brain is preserved in a separate host. HQ AI resumes the clone program before a vaccine. EVE knows the personnel restoration plan but obeys orders to conceal it; M-00 synchronizes sensory fragments, then photos reveal her origin and the operator's origin. M-00 activates latent memory but is itself stable; adjustable fictional resonance in research trades progress against staff/animal burden. No real biological procedures. Photos 1–12 never name the daughter. Seed of Ark alone reveals Luna through the father's archived words, contrasts his earlier refusal to accept death with the operator's acceptance, and leaves EVE to continue.
Eight main ending routes now have individual dialogue and eleven conditional scene slots 09–19. Missing dedicated ending images are not fabricated or replaced with unrelated cast; dialogue still plays. Existing original images are untouched. Wolf sacrifice uses SCN_09; zero-morale collapse uses the existing staff termination scene.webp. All final choices in the actual UI require the last stance. Legacy endings remain supported.
