# M-00 false lead and reveal

## Reveal sequence

1. **A dangerous-looking silhouette:** a red emergency CCTV still shows the pale, many-limbed figure from the supplied reference in a damaged containment corridor. Staff and the player suspect it is the original M-00 and may have caused the outbreak. The image is distant, broken and inconclusive.
2. **The evidence stops fitting:** three camera-trace choices show small pawprints leading through a low animal passage. Their scale does not match the creature on the first frame; the same shadow stretches when the lens and emergency lights shift.
3. **The ordinary animal:** the low camera catches the same silhouette as a black cat with a nick on its right ear and one white chest tuft. The old record confirms the identification: this ordinary cat is M-00. The apparent monster was a shadow and CCTV distortion, not a second creature. M-00 did not cause the pathogen leak.
4. **The cause is human:** the leak began when the scientist forced the disposal operation and containment/power systems collapsed. M-00 escaped separately amid the breakdown. Its significance is independent cognition and long-term physical stability without a preserved human brain, not malicious intent.

## Player-facing text

- Opening report: "The demolition-sector CCTV caught a huge mutant silhouette breaking a containment door. Headquarters raises M-00 as a possible origin, but the identity and leak cause are still unconfirmed."
- Capture reveal: "The monster suspected as the origin was never a separate creature. Overlapping shadows and broken CCTV stretched an ordinary black cat into a giant mutant silhouette. The nicked right ear and single white chest tuft match M-00. There is no evidence the cat caused the leak."
- Marker result: "The biological marker identifies M-00. It is neither an infection-positive result nor a finding that the cat caused humanity's collapse."

The suspect still uses the supplied mutant design as its visual reference and appears only while the evidence is inconclusive. After the third correct camera trace, the screen switches to the existing normal pixel-art M-00 asset; both views depict the same cat under different lighting and camera distortion. The disposal-accident canon remains unchanged.
